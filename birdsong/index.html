Our care

Memory care, in a house rather than a facility.

Some people need more than help getting through the day. Tap any of these to see how we handle it at Birdsong.

Memory care

The day runs the same way every day, on purpose. The same faces, the same rhythm, meals at the same table. When someone cannot hold on to what happened an hour ago, a predictable day is the thing that makes them feel safe. We do not correct or argue. We redirect gently and move on.

Dementia care

Birdsong is our dementia home, and it is set up for it. Six residents in a quiet house, low noise, nothing startling. We watch for the small changes that come before the big ones, and we keep families in the loop rather than waiting for something to go wrong.

Behavioral support

A calm, low-stimulation household, and a team who look for what is behind a behavior before responding to it. Most agitation has a reason: pain, noise, tiredness, a change in routine. We look for the reason first.

Respite care

Short stays are welcome. If you care for someone at home and you need a week for surgery, for a trip, or simply to rest, call and we will talk through what is possible. Families come back to us for this again and again.

Medications and health

Every dose given on time and recorded, refills handled, and appointments coordinated with each resident's own doctors. We work with the visiting nurse and the care team so nothing falls between people.

Everyday living

Gentle help with bathing, dressing, grooming and getting around safely. Home-cooked meals shared at the table. Clean rooms, laundry taken care of, and company through the day, which matters as much as any of the rest of it.

The home

A real log house on a quiet street.

Not a facility, not a wing of a building. Warm wood, six bedrooms, and a garden path that flowers every spring.

The approach

You arrive through a garden.

The path to the door is lined with dogwood and roses. It is a small thing, but it is the first thing a family sees, and it tells them what kind of place this is before anyone says a word.

Where the day happens

Everyone eats at the same table.

Meals are made fresh in the kitchen and eaten together. The clock, the calendar and the day's date are on the wall where everyone can see them, because for someone with memory loss those small anchors matter.

Their own room

A room to settle into.

Each resident has their own space, and we encourage families to bring what makes it theirs. Photographs, a favourite chair, the blanket from home. It helps more than people expect.

Built for safety

A bathroom that works for the people who use it.

Step-free shower, grab rails, a raised seat and room to help someone without crowding them. This is usually the first thing a case manager asks about, and the first thing we show them.

Questions families ask

The things people want to know.

Do you specialize in memory care and dementia?

Yes. Birdsong is our memory care and dementia home. The house is quiet, the routine is the same every day, and the caregivers are the same faces, which is what makes the day feel safe for someone living with memory loss.

How is an adult family home different from assisted living?

An adult family home is a licensed private residence caring for a small number of residents. Birdsong is licensed for six, in a real log home. It means far more one-to-one attention than a large facility can give, and it feels like a house because it is one.

Do you offer respite care?

Yes. If you care for someone at home and need a short stay covered, for surgery, a trip, or simply to rest, call and we will talk it through. Families come back to us for this again and again.

Do you accept Medicaid?

Yes. Birdsong accepts private pay and Medicaid, and we hold an Expanded Community Services contract, which covers residents whose needs are higher than a standard placement.

How many residents live at Birdsong?

Up to six. Keeping the home small is exactly what makes the individual attention possible.

Can we come and see the home first?

Please do. Visits are welcome by appointment. Call Mel on (253) 740-0890 and she will find a time that suits your family.
