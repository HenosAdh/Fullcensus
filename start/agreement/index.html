Client: your name, owner of your home, an adult family home in Washington.

1. What I am building for you

For a single flat fee, I design, build and launch a complete online presence for your home:

  • A custom website, designed, written and built to help families find you, with a contact form and tap to call.
  • Your content, written for you. I turn your story, your care details and your photos into clear, warm words.
  • Domain and business email in your name, for example hello@yourhome.com. The first year is included and you own them.
  • Google Business Profile, created and set up so you come up on Google Search and Maps.
  • Local search setup, so families searching your area can find you.
  • A free Dear Care Home directory listing.
  • Lead routing, so every inquiry is emailed to you instantly and nothing is missed.
  • Printed cards and brochures, if they are part of your quote.

If your home is not licensed yet, I build now and fill in your home name and DSHS licence number as they are confirmed, at no extra cost. Google can only verify your profile once you are licensed and operating.

2. Timeline

First draft: three days from the day we start, on a staging link, so you see the thing before anyone else does.

Website live: the date we agree. It goes in writing here before you pay anything, and it holds provided I begin on receipt of the first payment and you send me your photos and details.

Printing and mailing time belongs to the printer, so a print date is my best effort rather than a guarantee.

4. Photos

Send whatever photos you have. Phone photos are perfectly fine. I do not photograph your home myself. If you want professional photography I will refer you to a photographer and you would pay them directly, outside this agreement.

5. Revisions

Any time you want to add new photos, update your information or change details, I make those edits at no extra cost, for as long as you have the site. The fee is one time. You will not be billed again. Not included: building a website for another one of your homes, or paid ad campaigns. Those are quoted separately.

6. What I need from you
  • Photos of the home, the rooms, the common areas and the outside. I will guide you.
  • Your basic details: care types, private pay or Medicaid, open beds, and anything you want families to know.
  • Your home name and DSHS licence number, if they are not settled yet.

The timeline depends on these. If they are late, the launch date moves.

7. Who owns what

You own your website, your domain name, your content and photos, your Google Business Profile and your business email. They are yours to keep, permanently.

I keep my own templates, methods and process.

8. Results

I build a professional online presence and work hard to help families find you, but I cannot promise a specific number of calls, tours or move ins. That depends on your market, your home, your pricing and things outside my control. What I promise is a quality, professional job.

9. Term

This is a one time project, not a subscription. Once the site is launched the build is complete and there are no ongoing charges unless you choose an add on. The first payment is non refundable once work has begun.

10. Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Washington.
