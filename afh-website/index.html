Questions owners ask me

Short answers

How much does it cost?

It is one price, paid one time. Message me your home’s name and I will tell you the full price the same day, before any work starts. There are no surprise costs later.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. You pay one time. The only cost later is your domain name, once a year. You pay that to the domain company, not to me.

Who owns the website and the domain?

You do. Everything is in your name. If you stop working with me, you keep all of it.

I already have a website. Can you make it better?

Yes. You keep your domain and your email. I build the new website and move it over, so families never see it go down.

How long does it take?

Your first draft is guaranteed in 3 to 5 business days after I have your photos and your details. You look at it, tell me what to change, and then it goes live.

What do you need from me?

Photos of your home, your DSHS licence number, the care you offer, and the phone number families should call. I write the words for you.

I have more than one home.

Tell me how many. The price for each home is lower when you have two or more.

Do you take a fee when a resident moves in?

No. I do not place residents and I do not charge placement fees. I never sell your information.

Who will I talk to?

Me. I answer the phone myself and I do the work myself.
