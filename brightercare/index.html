Our Story

The care I grew up in.

"This isn't a business I started. It's a life I was shaped by."

My father ran an adult family home, and I grew up inside it. I absorbed what it means to care for every person with patience and dignity.

It was never a job in our house. It was simply how we lived. I stepped into it with open eyes and a full heart, and trained as a licensed CNA to do it right.

Brighter Care is built on everything those years taught me, carried into a home of my own.

Ebrima Ceesay · Owner and provider
