Known by name

Every Resident has a story, Not a Number.

At Olympia Senior Care, Residents are known by name, not number. Personalized shadow boxes display treasured photos and keepsakes, creating familiar spaces that honor each Resident’s life, memories, and individuality.

Because every story deserves to be remembered.

Life enrichment

Montessori memory care, in plain terms.

We build the day around what a resident can still do, never around what they have lost.

1

Something to do together

Baking, folding laundry, planting the raised beds. Sunday tea, Thursday happy hour, birthdays, and family dinners every quarter.

2

Something to keep them moving

Setting a table or carrying linens does more for balance than an exercise class, and it does not feel like therapy. Outings to the senior center when they want them.

3

Something worth thinking about

Puzzles and music chosen for the person, at a pace that will not defeat them. Pet therapy, visits from children, and church groups for anyone who wants them.

What is cooking

Nothing here comes out of a packet.

Three meals and two snacks a day, cooked in this kitchen from a six-week menu, so nothing repeats week to week. A registered dietitian signs off on every rotation, and we cook to whatever your doctor has ordered.
