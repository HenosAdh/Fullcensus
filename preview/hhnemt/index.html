Good to know

Questions

Is this an ambulance?

No. We carry people who are medically stable and just need a safe way to get there. If someone needs treatment on the way, that is an ambulance. Call 911.

Does Medicare pay for my ride?

Usually not. Original Medicare covers an ambulance when it is medically necessary, but not an ordinary ride to an appointment. Some Medicare Advantage plans include a few trips a year, so it is worth checking the number on your card. Michigan Medicaid is different and does cover rides to covered care.

Can you take a power wheelchair?

Yes. Tell us which kind of chair when you book, standard, transport or power, so we allow enough time.

How far ahead should I book?

A day or two is ideal, and standing weekly trips can be set up once and left alone. Need something today? Ring anyway. We will tell you honestly whether the van is free.

Can someone ride with me?

Yes, and there is no charge for them. Just say so when you book so we keep the seat.

Will the driver help me out of the house?

Yes. We come to the door, not the curb, and we take you inside at the other end rather than leaving you in a parking lot.

Do you carry stretcher patients?

No, not at the moment. We take walking riders, walker users and wheelchair riders. If a stretcher is what you need we will tell you straight away and point you somewhere that has one.

Where are you based?

Greater Lansing. We cover Ingham, Eaton and Clinton counties, and we will drive further for a specialist appointment in Ann Arbor, Detroit or Grand Rapids.
