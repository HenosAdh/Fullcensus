Rides we provide

Every trip that keeps your care on track

If it is a medical appointment, we can get you there. No ambulance, no emergency — just a clean, comfortable ride with someone who helps you in and out.

Doctor appointments

Primary care, specialists, labs, imaging and follow-ups. We plan the pick-up around your appointment time, not the other way round.

Dialysis treatment

Same days, same times, every week. Dialysis is the ride you cannot miss, so it is the one we build the schedule around.

Physical therapy

Hospital discharges

Pharmacy trips

And more

Adult day programs, dental and vision visits, infusion centers, wound care, family visits to a loved one in care. If you are not sure, just ask.

What makes the ride different

A ride-share driver drops you at the curb. We do not.

Most of the people we carry cannot manage a curb, a step or a heavy door on their own. That is the whole job, and it is the part a taxi app was never built to do.

You stay in your chair

A side-entry ramp and a proper four-point securement system, so a wheelchair rider rolls straight in. No lifting, no transfers, no struggle.

From the door, not the curb

We come to the door, walk you out, and take you all the way in at the other end — to reception, not the parking lot.

The same driver, on time

Small enough that you are not a number. You get a driver who learns your name, your chair and your routine.

Common questions

Questions families ask us

Is this an ambulance?

No. Non-emergency medical transportation is for people who are medically stable and simply need a safe, accessible ride to care. If someone needs medical treatment on the way, that is an ambulance — call 911.

Can you take a wheelchair?

Yes. Our van has a side-entry ramp and a securement system, so you stay in your own chair for the whole journey. Tell us the type of chair when you book — standard, transport or power — so we bring the right van and allow enough time.

How far ahead do I need to book?

The more notice the better, and a day or two ahead is ideal for a routine appointment. But if you need a ride today, call us anyway — we will tell you honestly whether we can cover it.

How do I pay, and does my insurance cover it?

Call us with your plan details before you book and we will tell you exactly what it will cost you and what, if anything, your plan covers. Many Michigan Medicaid members get their rides arranged through their health plan's transportation line — if that is you, we will point you to the right number so you are not out of pocket.

Can someone ride with me?

Yes. A family member, caregiver or escort is welcome at no extra charge, as long as there is room in the van. Tell us when you book so we keep a seat.

Will the driver help me out of the house?

Yes. That is the difference between us and a taxi. We come to the door, help you out, help you into the building at the other end, and wait with you if you need us to.

What are your hours?

Seven days a week, 5:00am to 10:00pm. Early dialysis and late discharges are exactly why we start at five and finish at ten.

Do you carry stretcher patients?

Not at the moment. We carry ambulatory riders, walker users and wheelchair riders. If a stretcher is needed we will tell you honestly and help you find the right service.
