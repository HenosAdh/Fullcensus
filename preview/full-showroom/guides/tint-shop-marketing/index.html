How tint shops get found in 2026

368,000 Americans search "window tinting near me" every month. Here's exactly how that buyer picks a shop, and the five assets that decide whether they pick yours.

By Henos Adhana, Full Showroom · Updated August 2026 · Search data: Google Ads / DataForSEO

The demand is not your problem

Every month in the US, Google logs about 368,000 searches for "window tinting near me", another 90,500 for "tint shop near me", and 49,500 for "paint protection film". Those aren't researchers — "near me" means they want a bay this week. Your problem is never demand. It's whether that buyer ever sees your name.

How the buyer actually chooses

  1. They search once — usually on a phone, often in a parking lot.
  2. They look at the map pack — the top three pins. Most never scroll past it.
  3. They compare reviews — count and recency, in about five seconds.
  4. They tap the website — to check prices, photos of real work, and whether the shop feels legit enough for a $500–$2,000 job.
  5. They call or book — the first shop that survives steps 2–4 gets the job.

Miss any step and the buyer falls through to the next shop. A five-star shop with no website loses at step 4 every single day — invisibly.

What to fix this week (free)

  • Claim your Google Business Profile — google.com/business, 20 minutes. Add hours, services with prices, and 10+ photos of real installs.
  • Ask your last five happy customers for a review — text them the direct review link. Recency matters more than totals.
  • Google yourself from a phone: "tint shop near me", "window tinting [your city]". Whatever shows up instead of you is your real competitor list.
