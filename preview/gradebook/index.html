How the grade is built

Six things decide whether a house can hold residents

The grade is my read on the property's fit for adult family home use. It is never a comment on the price, the listing or the agent, and the licensor always has the final say.

25 points

Bathrooms

WAC 388-76-10780 wants one toilet, sink and bathing fixture per five people in the home. Residents and live-in staff both count. This is the most common killer.

20 points

Evacuation

WAC 388-76-10865 requires everyone to be able to get out with the staff actually on shift. Bedrooms upstairs is the single biggest capacity question.

20 points

Bedrooms

Enough rooms for the residents you want plus a room for a live-in caregiver, if you plan to have one.

15 points

Accessibility

Step-free entry, door and hallway width, turning room. Newer builds and single levels start ahead. Everything here is retrofittable, at a price.

10 points

Site

Lot size, staff parking, and whether an aid car can get in and turn around at two in the morning.

10 points

Market

Drive time to the nearest hospital, because discharge planners are the referral source most homes ignore, and the depth of the county around it.

Your NWMLS filter

Structure

Residential, single family. Bedrooms 4+. Baths 2+. Square feet 1,900–4,000. Lot 5,000+.

Style

Prefer 1 Story and 1 Story w/ Bsmnt. Two-story is not excluded, it just starts twenty points down and is worth watching for the rare main-floor primary.

Status

Active plus Back on Market, listed in the last 7 days. Back on Market is where the motivated sellers hide.

Read the remarks

Search remarks for ADU, main floor primary, ramp, wide hall, group home, care, WABO. The last one is rare and worth a same-day call.

What this page is not

Not a valuation, not a listing review, not a comment on any agent. It grades a building against one use. The licensor always has the final say.

MLS content

No listing photos and no MLS data are republished here. The schematics are drawn from the numbers. Link to the listing rather than reposting it, and credit the listing brokerage.

Naming

Name the address on strong grades where it helps a buyer. Withhold it on weak grades, and never pair a low grade with a price, a photo or a brokerage.
