How far is this house from a licensed home?

Five houses a week, one from each county that actually holds Washington's adult family homes, graded on a single question: how far is this building from a licensed home? Not a valuation. Not a listing review. Just the six things the state will measure.

5,293 homes in these 5 counties 86% of the state Graded S to F

How the grade is built

Six things decide whether a house can hold residents

The grade is a read on the property's fit for adult family home use. It is never a comment on the price, the listing or the agent, and the licensor always has the final say.

25 points

Bathrooms

WAC 388-76-10780 wants one toilet, sink and bathing fixture per five people in the home. Residents and live-in staff both count. This is the most common killer.

20 points

Evacuation

WAC 388-76-10865 requires everyone to be able to get out with the staff actually on shift. Bedrooms upstairs is the single biggest capacity question.

20 points

Bedrooms

Enough rooms for the residents you want, plus a room for a live-in caregiver if you plan to have one.

15 points

Accessibility

Step-free entry, door and hallway width, turning room. Newer builds and single levels start ahead. All of it is retrofittable, at a price.

10 points

Site

Lot size, staff parking, and whether an aid car can get in and turn around at two in the morning.

10 points

Market

Drive time to the nearest hospital, because discharge planners are the referral source most homes ignore, and the depth of the county around it.

The search that feeds it

The NWMLS filter

Save this once as a recurring search per county and the Gradebook writes itself every Monday. Tuned to surface conversion candidates, not finished homes.

Structure

Residential, single family. Bedrooms 4+. Baths 2+. Square feet 1,900 to 4,000. Lot 5,000+.

Style

Prefer 1 Story and 1 Story w/ Bsmnt. Two-story is not excluded, it just starts twenty points down and is worth watching for the rare main-floor primary.

Status

Active plus Back on Market, listed in the last 7 days. Back on Market is where the motivated sellers hide.

Read the remarks

Search remarks for ADU, main floor primary, ramp, wide hall, group home, care, WABO. The last one is rare and worth a same-day call.

What this is not

Not a valuation, not a listing review, and not a comment on any agent. It grades a building against one use. The licensor always has the final say.

Listing content

No listing photos and no MLS data are republished here. Every schematic is drawn from the numbers. Listings are linked, never reposted, and the listing brokerage is credited.
